# Plans, billing and refunds

Lumen Fleet is billed monthly or annually. All prices are in US dollars and
exclude tax. Every plan includes mission replay, alerts, and community support.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly price | Robots | Included API calls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $99 | 10 | 250,000 |
| Growth | $499 | 50 | 2,000,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | 20,000,000 |

Growth overage costs **$0.40 per additional 1,000 API calls**. Starter overage
costs $0.70 per 1,000 calls. API calls beyond the Enterprise allowance are billed
at the contracted rate; Enterprise customers can also add dedicated capacity.

## Monitoring usage

The billing dashboard shows usage with a six-hour delay. Owners receive an email
at 80%, 100%, and 120% of the included allowance. Set a hard monthly cap under
Billing → Limits; requests are rejected with HTTP 402 after the cap is reached.

## Cancellation and refunds

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the
end of the current billing period. Monthly fees are non-refundable. Annual
subscriptions receive a 15% discount and can be refunded on a pro-rated basis
within **30 days** of purchase. After 30 days, annual plans can be cancelled at
renewal but are not refunded. Add-on usage is never refundable.
